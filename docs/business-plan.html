Skyline Realty — 10-Year Plan

From an AI-powered channel partner to a ₹8,800 Cr ($1B) real-estate company, 100% owned by the two founders · Nov 2026

1. The goal, written down so it can be measured

By December 2036: a company worth ₹8,800 Cr (about $1B at ₹88/$), owned 100% by you and your wife. At normal valuations for profitable real-estate companies (about 15× profit) that means about ₹600 Cr profit a year from about ₹15,000 Cr of homes sold a year.

The honest part first. No plan can make a $1B outcome "extremely likely". Very few Indian real-estate firms have got there, and almost all of them took outside investors. What this plan can do:
  • make every stage profitable on its own, so a miss never sinks the company;
  • let measured gates, not hope, decide when to spend on the next step.
Even if the top target is missed, you keep a ₹200–500 Cr business that you own completely. That is how the odds are tilted in your favour.

2. Why the current plan alone cannot reach $1B

A channel partner earns 2–3% of each sale and depends on builders for everything: stock, price and payment. Brokerage firms are valued low for that reason.

To be worth $1B as a pure broker you would need about ₹60,000 Cr of sales a year. That is far beyond any single broker in India today, and it would need hundreds of salespeople across many cities. It is not a high-probability path.

So the AI sales engine we built is the starting point, not the end. It is the cheapest, most measurable way to win buyers. That ability is worth far more when you control the product.

7. Growing to $1B while keeping 100%

Money that doesn't cost you shares

  • Profits from distribution, reinvested (years 1–4)
  • JDA: the landowner's land is paid in flats, not cash
  • Buyer advances through the RERA project account
  • Construction finance from banks or NBFCs, secured on the project
  • Partners in one project only: an investor can fund a single project company for a share of that project's profit. The main company stays 100% yours.
  • Later: bonds or structured debt at company level

What to avoid

  • Selling shares of the main company, for any reason
  • Buying land outright with cash in the early years
  • Unlimited personal guarantees
  • Fixed costs (offices, salaries) ahead of proven bookings
  • Launching an own project before the demand book shows pre-sales
  • Note: a stock-market listing (IPO) would mean selling shares. At 100% ownership, "$1B" means a private company earning ~₹600 Cr a year that belongs only to you.

Company structure — do this in year 1 (with your CA and lawyer)

  • A partnership firm carries unlimited personal liability, which is dangerous once you develop property. It is also harder to finance and to hand on.
  • Move the operations into a private limited company (Tiruana Pvt Ltd, or a new "Skyline Realty Pvt Ltd") owned 100% by you both.
  • Keep the Skyline Realty brand for customers.
  • Each own project goes into its own company under it.
  • The RERA agent registrations will need to be in the company's name: plan the timing so no sale falls in a gap.

  1. Open 10-yr plan: are we on pace? Which Gate-1 line is weakest? That becomes this week's single focus.
  2. Reports: move ad money from the worst channel (cost per booking) to the best.

The one-line strategy

Win buyers more cheaply than anyone with AI, use that to win exclusive builder deals, use the buyer data to build your own projects without buying land, and grow only through gates you have proven. Never sell a share.
