AI + Human Sales System — how a buyer goes from enquiry to booking, and who does what

Who does it: AI assistantSalespersonSite team You (management)Buyer / builder
1
Enquiry arrives Buyer
  • From a landing page, 99acres / MagicBricks / Housing email, Meta lead ad, Click-to-WhatsApp ad, walk-in or referral.
  • The system creates the lead, removes duplicates, scores it 0–100 and assigns the salesperson for that city.
2
First contact — within 1 minute AI
  • WhatsApp: "Hi, I'm Skyline Realty's assistant, working with Anitha…" — always says it is an AI assistant.
  • Asks one qualifying question and creates an intro call task due in 10 minutes, with a brief.
3
Intro call Salesperson
  • Calls using the brief in the portal; logs the call in one tap (or WhatsApp: CALLED C3 notes).
  • The AI continues in writing: "As we discussed…", sends matching homes, floor plans, cost sheet, EMI.
  • Reminders if the call is late: +20 min, +60 min; you are told after 3 hours.
4
Understand needs & match homes AI Salesperson
  • Budget, size, area, ready / under construction, loan, who decides.
  • AI searches all inventory (own + partner builders, Chennai + Bangalore) and recommends the best 2–3 real units.
5
Book the site visit AI
  • Offers two time slots; books the site host; partner projects: buyer is registered with the builder in writing first (commission protection).
  • Reminders to buyer, site host and salesperson: day before + 2 hours before. Salesperson gets a confirmation call task.
6
Site visit Site team
  • Site app: Confirm → Customer arrived (salesperson told instantly) → show the right homes → fill the 2-minute report.
7
Closing plan AI Salesperson
  • AI turns the visit report into a conversion strategy: motivators, objections + answers, best unit, price plan, next steps.
  • Same-day post-visit call by the salesperson, following the strategy.
8
Price & negotiation — your approval You
  • The AI never gives a discount itself. It sends you the request with the buyer's ask, its reasons, your floor margin and commission.
  • You reply APPROVE 7 · COUNTER 7 95L · REJECT 7. Approved price is told on a call first, then in writing. Unit area is never negotiable.
9
Booking & advance You Buyer
  • Buyer agrees → you CONFIRM B3 → AI sends the official bank details (yours, or the partner builder's).
  • When money reaches the bank, you reply PAID B3 2L UTR123 → flat booked, unit off sale, commission recorded.

If the buyer goes quiet: useful WhatsApp → reconnect call → WhatsApp with something new → call at a different time → "reply 1 to continue, 2 to pause" → nurture every 2 weeks. Any reply restarts the conversation.

Your daily routine (10–20 min)

  • 9 AM: read the WhatsApp morning summary
  • Approve / counter price requests (WhatsApp or Overview)
  • Confirm bookings; mark advances received after checking the bank
  • Glance at the Overview checklist and "Leads needing you"
  • Weekly: enter ad spend (Reports), read Learning, update inventory

Salesperson's day

  • Open the sales portal: Calls to make now first (red = overdue)
  • Each buyer page shows the current step and the AI brief
  • Call → log it (outcome + notes) → the AI follows up in writing
  • Approve or edit AI drafts
  • Do confirmation calls the day before visits

Site team's visit

  • Day before: reminder with buyer details + link
  • Tap Called & confirmed
  • On arrival tap They have arrived
  • Show the homes listed in the app
  • Right after: fill the visit report (or "Customer didn't come")

Partner builder

  • Receives a written client registration before each visit (R-number, with our RERA number)
  • Replies ACK R12 (or DISPUTE R12 reason)
  • Hosts the visit at their site; pays commission by their terms — tracked on the Commissions page

Safety rules built into the system

Floor prices are never shown to the AI or buyersEvery discount or extra needs your approval first
Unit area is never negotiableBank details are fixed text from Settings — the AI can't type them
A flat is "booked" only when you confirm the money arrived"STOP" from a buyer stops all messages instantly
Price, booking, payment and complaints always use the best AI modelComplaints / legal questions pause the AI and alert you
