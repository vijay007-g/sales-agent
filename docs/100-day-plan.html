2. What makes version 2 more likely to succeed

1Focus beats spread. In 2–3 Chennai micro-markets, builders, bankers and buyers start recognising Skyline within weeks.
2Hire the salesperson first (joins day 22). They make the calls and handle visits; you handle builders, closing and approvals, the things only you can do.
3Lead with ready-to-move and near-possession homes. Buyers decide faster when they can see the finished flat and plan a move-in date. Completed projects with an occupancy certificate also attract no GST, a strong talking point (confirm per project).
4Warm channels before paid ones: your network, referrals, home-loan branch managers and corporate HR sessions. Cheaper leads, higher trust, faster bookings. Ads add volume on top.
5Builder trust on autopilot: the weekly builder report (now built into the system) goes out every Wednesday from week 5. That's how pilots and better slabs are earned.
6Speed and visit rate: call within 15 minutes, free cab pickup, weekend slots, and you at every visit for the first 3 weekends to learn what closes.
7Spend only behind proof: ads rise only while cost per booking stays below 30% of earnings, and salesperson #2 joins only above 120 leads a month.
Bangalore and the Karnataka RERA registration: it is in your wife's name. Before any Bangalore activity, ask your CA and a RERA consultant whether the firm can trade there under her registration while she is a sleeping partner, or whether the firm (or the new Pvt Ltd) should register in Karnataka itself. Until then: no Bangalore ads or registrations. The authority letter (day 4) covers the documents she would otherwise have to sign.

6. CP plan with builders — the strategy

Most channel partners are one of 100–300 brokers a builder works with, competing on discounts. Skyline's plan is to become the builder's most reliable sales partner in each focus market, then convert that into preferred and exclusive deals. We earn that with data, not discounts.

The 12-point CP agreement checklist

  1. Commission % and volume slabs in writing (e.g. 2% base → 2.5% after 3 bookings/quarter → 3% after 6)
  2. Basis: % of what? Basic price or agreement value; excluding GST, registration, maintenance, car park?
  3. Payout trigger and days (often: agreement registered + 10–20% collected; payment within 30–45 days)
  4. Client tagging: how (email/portal), ACK within 24 h, validity 60–90 days
  5. Disputes: first registered CP wins; a buyer walking in directly within validity is still ours
  6. Cancellation / clawback: only if cancelled before payout; no clawback after
  7. Spot incentives and offers passed to us in writing
  8. Price discipline: the builder's team won't undercut a buyer we registered
  9. Site support: site staff for our visits, sample flat, brochures, floor plans, price sheets
  10. Invoicing: GST 18% on commission; TDS under Sec. 194H (confirm the current rate with your CA); NEFT
  11. Buyer data used only for registration; no poaching of our buyers for other projects
  12. Term, termination, jurisdiction (Chennai courts)
Red flags, walk away: fee or deposit for empanelment · project not RERA-registered · stalled site · reports of delayed CP payouts · builder wants buyer details before agreeing to protect your registration · "commission only after the full payment".

7. How to approach builders

Ways in, best first

  1. Warm introduction: bankers (home-loan managers know every builder), architects, other CPs, friends in the industry. Best response rate.
  2. Walk into the project sales office and ask for the CP manager. Weekday mornings are quiet; weekends are busy.
  3. CP meets and launch events: builders invite CPs at launches. Get added to each builder's CP WhatsApp group.
  4. CREDAI chapters and property expos in Chennai: meet many builders in one day.
  5. LinkedIn to Sales Heads / VP Sales, short message + one-pager.
  6. Cold call the CP desk numbers listed on websites and hoardings.

Who to meet, for what

CP managerEmpanelment, agreement, tagging
Sales head / GM salesPilots, slabs, slow inventory
CRM / accounts headPayout timing, invoices, agreement dates
MD / founderPreferred and exclusive deals (from month 3)

What builders actually want

  • Steady bookings, especially on slow towers and configurations
  • Footfall that is qualified, not time-wasters
  • Price discipline: CPs who don't pass commission as a discount
  • No disputes and no extra work for their team
  • Honest feedback on why buyers don't buy

Skyline's four promises to a builder (say these in every meeting)

1Every enquiry answered in 5 minutes and followed up until a decision. AI + a salesperson, 7 days a week, every follow-up logged.
2Only qualified buyers visit. Budget, timeline and loan readiness are checked first, and every buyer is registered with you before the visit.
3We never pass our commission as a discount. Every price goes through management approval. Your pricing stays protected.
4A weekly report: buyers registered, visits, and what buyers say about your price, product and competitors. Free market intelligence.

8. Objections, pilot and mandate

Handling builder objections

They sayYou say
"We already have 200 CPs.""That's why we focus on qualified buyers and reporting, not volume of brokers. Judge us on bookings in 60 days. There's no cost to you unless we deliver."
"You're a new company.""New firm, experienced founder, RERA-registered. Our system answers every buyer in 5 minutes. Let our first 60 days speak; you lose nothing by empanelling us."
"Commission is fixed at 2%.""Agreed for now. Can we add a slab if we cross 3 bookings a quarter? It only costs you when we outperform."
"CPs give away commission as discount.""Not us. Every price is approved by management, and we can put no-discount-passing in the agreement."
"Payout only after full registration.""Can we split it: 50% at agreement registration and 50% at 20% collection? That keeps us both aligned."
"Send the buyer, we'll see.""Happy to. We just need the registration ACK before the visit, so there's never a dispute. It protects your team's time too."
"No exclusive deals.""Not asking for one now. Let's start with a 60-day pilot on one tower; exclusivity only if the numbers justify it."
"How do I know you won't take our buyers elsewhere?""A registered buyer stays registered with you, and we show you every registered buyer's status in the weekly report."
